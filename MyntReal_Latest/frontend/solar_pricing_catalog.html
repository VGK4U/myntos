Installation Roadmap

Seamless 4-Step Installation Journey

From initial technical site audit to net-metering switch-on in under 21 business days.

01

Technical Site Audit

Our certified engineer inspects your rooftop, conducts 3D shadow analysis, checks electrical load capacity, and prepares custom layout drawings.

02

Engineering & DISCOM Approval

We file your application on the national PM Surya Ghar portal, secure DISCOM feasibility clearance, and lock in your ₹78,000 DBT subsidy reservation.

03

Certified Installation

Heavy-duty HDG mounting structure erection, Tier-1 Mono PERC panel installation, inverter cabling, and dual-pole DC earthing pit testing.

04

Net Metering & Subsidy Release

DISCOM installs the bi-directional net meter, synchronizes grid generation, and the central government DBT subsidy credits straight to your bank account.
