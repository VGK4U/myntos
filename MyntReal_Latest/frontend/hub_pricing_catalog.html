Frequently Asked Commercial Questions (Investor FAQs)

Key contractual, tax, operational, and warranty policies addressed for prospective franchise partners.

The 12% Hub Margin is your contractual showroom wholesale margin earned whenever an EV is sold from your dealer stock to a retail buyer or sub-dealer. When your in-house team actively closes the retail customer directly, you also receive the 10.5% VGK4U Direct Sale Commission, yielding a combined total of 22.5% direct customer sale spread (~₹12,850 per vehicle ex-tax).

This complies strictly with standard automotive dealership accounting and Indian statutory taxation. Customer retail prices include 5% vehicle GST. Since GST is collected on behalf of the government and passed through, dealer commissions are legally and contractually computed on the ex-tax base price (Retail Price ÷ 1.05).

MyntReal central engineering executes 100% of the solar EPC delivery. Your Hub is solely responsible for lead generation, customer relationship, and booking. Our certified engineers conduct the structural site audit, secure net-metering approvals from the local DISCOM, and erect the solar plant. You earn your guaranteed 3.5% (or 10% on direct sale) with zero technical execution liability.

Yes. Under Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), electric vehicles with a maximum speed of 25 km/h and electric motor power under 250W are classified as Low-Speed Non-RTO Vehicles. They do not require RTO road tax, vehicle registration plates, or a driving license, making them extraordinarily popular for students, daily commuters, and rural delivery workers.

Automotive LFP batteries carry a 2+1 Years Warranty; Graphene batteries carry a 9-Month Warranty; Smart Chargers carry a 9-Month Warranty; and the vehicle chassis carries a 3-Year Comprehensive Warranty. Central OEM handles warranty claims directly. In the event of a cell or BMS fault, the component is replaced or calibrated by the regional warehouse within 48 to 72 hours.
