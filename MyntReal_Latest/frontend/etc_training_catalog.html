Practical Curriculum

6 Core Competencies You Will Master

Every hour of your 36-hour training is engineered around real-world diagnostic competencies demanded by EV dealerships and independent service centers.

1. Live 2-Wheeler Teardown & Assembly

Full physical dismantling of high-speed and slow-speed electric scooters. Learn chassis mechanical layout, suspension, braking systems, and electrical harness routing.

Outcome: Confidently disassemble and reassemble any commercial EV 2-wheeler.

2. Basics of Battery & Chargers

Understand Lithium-ion & LFP cell arrangements, series/parallel grouping, Battery Management System (BMS) fundamentals, thermal safety cutoffs, and charger calibration.

Outcome: Inspect battery packs, verify voltage balance, and diagnose smart chargers safely.

3. BLDC Motor & Hall Sensor Diagnostics

In-depth study of Brushless DC (BLDC) hub and mid-drive motors. Master 3-phase stator testing, permanent magnet inspection, Hall sensor troubleshooting, and phase wire continuity.

Outcome: Diagnose motor jamming, stuttering, Hall sensor failures, and replace stator coils.

4. Controller Tuning & Throttle Calibration

Master the electronic brain of the EV. Troubleshoot speed controllers, MOSFET switching, throttle Hall-effect sensors, electronic braking (E-ABS), and reverse gear wiring.

Outcome: Pinpoint blown controller circuits, program speed limits, and fix throttle lags.

5. Digital Multimeter & OBD Scanning

Hands-on testing with digital multimeters, clamp meters, and OBD scanner tools. Measure voltage drops, insulation resistance, DC-DC converter 12V outputs, and harness shorts.

Outcome: Isolate electrical fault codes in under 15 minutes using structured testing SOPs.

6. Workshop SOP & High Voltage Safety

Crucial automotive safety guidelines: high-voltage rubber gloves, insulated tool handling, battery fire prevention, ESD protection, and standard operating procedures (SOP).

Outcome: Operate accident-free workshop facilities adhering to international safety standards.
