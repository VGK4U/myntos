Frequently asked

You probably want to know…

Do I need a credit card for the 7-day free trial?

No. Sign up with your work email and we’ll provision your tenant for 7 days, free. You can test your modules, workflows, and document templates before paying a rupee.

What is the effective per-user cost across plans?

Your per-login cost drops automatically as your team grows: ₹1,999/user (1 Login), ₹1,749.50/user (2 Logins), ₹1,299.80/user (5 Logins), and ₹999.90/user/mo (10 Logins). Choosing Annual Billing gives you 2 months FREE (bringing the 10-Login plan down to just ₹833.25/user/mo).

Which plan includes Meta Ads Integration & WhatsApp API Auto Messaging?

WhatsApp Direct (1-on-1 CRM messaging) is included on every plan starting from 1 Login (₹1,999/mo). High-volume Meta/Facebook Ads Lead Sync and WhatsApp API Auto Messaging & Broadcasts are included in the 10 Logins Enterprise Suite (₹9,999/mo · ₹999.90/user) alongside Service Desk and Ved Member Referral Tracking.

Are WhatsApp conversation fees & telephony usage included in the plan price?

Your Zynova OS subscription covers software licensing, CRM, workflows, 1-click document generation, and module entitlements. Meta WhatsApp Business API conversation charges, telecom carrier calling minutes, and AI voice usage are billed transparently as per actual consumption.

How does pro-rata billing work when upgrading?

When you upgrade your login tier or add modules mid-cycle, we issue a single pro-rata adjustment invoice for the remaining days of your current billing period.

Can I run multiple GST legal entities and export to Tally/Zoho?

Yes. Each tenant can manage multiple GST-registered legal entities with HSN/SAC codes, tax breakdowns, and full audit logs.
