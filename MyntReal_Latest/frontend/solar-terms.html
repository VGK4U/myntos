2. Equipment Warranties & Manufacturer Guarantees

All solar photovoltaic modules, on-grid inverters, and key hardware components supplied through facilitated projects carry direct OEM manufacturer warranties:

  • 25-Year Limited Performance Warranty: Solar PV modules are backed by a 25-year linear performance warranty from their respective Tier-1 OEMs (guaranteeing minimum 90% power generation in the first 10–12 years and minimum 80% through year 25).
  • Inverter Product Warranty: On-grid string inverters are backed by a 5 to 8-year standard manufacturer warranty with extended service options available directly through the OEM.
  • Structural & Workmanship SLA: Physical structural fabrication, mounting, and cabling carry a minimum 2-year workmanship Service Level Agreement (SLA) provided by the designated local certified execution partner.

* Note: Warranty claims for hardware defects, panel degradation, or component failures must be processed in accordance with the warranty policies and service networks of the respective manufacturers.

4. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Central DBT Subsidy

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India, provides Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) subsidies under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana for residential rooftop solar installations:

  • Residential Subsidy Scale:
    • 1 kW System: ₹30,000 Central DBT Subsidy
    • 2 kW System: ₹60,000 Central DBT Subsidy
    • 3 kW and Higher (Residential): ₹78,000 Maximum Central DBT Subsidy
  • Commercial & Industrial Installations: Commercial, institutional, and industrial solar projects do not qualify for residential central DBT subsidies. However, commercial owners benefit from 40% Accelerated Depreciation under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and 18% GST Input Tax Credit (ITC).
  • Disbursement Timeline: Central DBT subsidies are transferred directly into the applicant's Aadhaar-seeded bank account by the central nodal agency following portal inspection and net-meter commissioning. MyntReal assists in filing and tracking documentation but is not responsible for nodal agency processing delays.

5. DISCOM Net-Metering & Generation Estimates

  • Grid Net-Metering Feasibility: Installation of bidirectional net meters, grid feasibility approvals, and tariff credit adjustments are governed entirely by the state electricity distribution company (DISCOM) regulations (e.g., APCPDCL, APSPDCL, TGSPDCL, TSSPDCL, BESCOM, TANGEDCO, etc.). Grid approvals depend on distribution transformer capacity and local distribution infrastructure.
  • Standard Test Condition (STC) Estimates: Unit generation estimates (e.g., ~360–420 units per month for a 3 kW solar rooftop system) are calculated based on Standard Test Conditions (1,000 W/m² irradiance at 25°C) and average regional solar insolation of 4.5 to 5.5 peak sun hours. Actual real-world generation is subject to local rooftop orientation (azimuth), tilt angle, shadow-free unobstructed area, seasonal rainfall, dust accumulation, and routine cleaning maintenance.
