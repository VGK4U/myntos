Customer variation, priority clean energy loans, and indicative calculation terms
Customer-to-Customer & Bank-to-Bank Variation
EMI amounts, interest rates (typically 7%–10% p.a.), loan tenures (up to 5 or 10 years), and collateral-free loan sanction vary strictly from customer to customer and bank to bank based on individual CIBIL / credit bureau score (typically 700+ required), monthly household income, banking transactions, debt-to-income ratio, KYC verification, and individual bank underwriting policies.
Lending Institutions
Rooftop solar loans are underwritten and disbursed by nationalized partner banks (State Bank of India - SBI, Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank - PNB, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, Indian Bank, Andhra Pragathi Grameena Bank - APGB, IREDA) and partner RBI-registered NBFCs under priority sector clean energy lending schemes.

3. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Central DBT Subsidy Policy

Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) guidelines and DBT disbursement process
Official Subsidy Scale
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) central subsidy is governed strictly by Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) guidelines: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and flat ₹78,000 maximum for 3 kW up to 10 kW residential rooftop systems.
Direct Bank Credit
Subsidies are credited directly into the applicant's Aadhaar-linked savings bank account through the National Solar Portal following DISCOM site inspection, commissioning verification, and net-meter activation.
Commercial & Industrial Establishments
Residential central DBT subsidies do not apply to commercial, institutional, or industrial connections. Commercial consumers benefit from Section 32 Accelerated Tax Depreciation (40%) and substantial operational tariff savings.

4. DISCOM Net-Metering & Estimated Solar Generation

Grid synchronization, transformer limits, and test conditions
Grid Synchronization & DISCOM Approvals
Installation of bi-directional net-meters and grid connection synchronization are subject to local power distribution company (DISCOM) approvals, distribution transformer (DTR) capacity availability, and state regulatory authority turnaround times.
Solar Generation Estimates
Quoted solar unit generation (approx. 4 to 4.5 units per day per kW, or 120–135 units/month) is calculated under Standard Test Conditions (STC) with unshaded south-facing rooftop installations. Actual generation may vary due to ambient temperature, seasonal weather, cloud cover, shadow patterns, and periodic panel cleaning.
